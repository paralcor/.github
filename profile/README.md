很长时间里，人们知道恒星很远，却量不出有多远。后来的办法不是靠近它，而是换个位置看它：相隔半年的两次观测，地球已经走到公转轨道的另一侧，近处的恒星相对遥远的背景，看起来挪动了极小的一点。量出这一点，就量出了距离。不必抵达群星，也可以丈量群星。

PARALCOR 取自 PARALLAX（视差）与 ALCOR（辅星），中文副名「隐星视差」。

<summary>English</summary>

For a long time, people knew the stars were far away but could not tell how far. The answer was not to get closer, but to look from somewhere else: six months apart, Earth is on the other side of its orbit, and a nearby star appears to shift ever so slightly against the distant background. Measure that shift, and you have the distance. You need not reach the stars to measure them.

PARALCOR comes from PARALLAX and ALCOR, the faint companion of Mizar in the Big Dipper. Its Chinese name is 帕拉珂.

PARALCOR builds software, AI applications and developer tools.
