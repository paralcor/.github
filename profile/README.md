## 名字的由来

PARALCOR 是 PARALLAX（视差）与 ALCOR（辅星）合成的词。

- **Alcor**：北斗开阳星（Mizar）旁一颗暗弱的伴星。
- **Parallax**：观察位置改变时，天体相对遥远背景的视位置变化。

很长时间里，人们知道恒星很远，却量不出有多远。后来的办法不是靠近它，而是换个位置看它：地球绕太阳公转，相隔半年的两次观测，位置相差约三亿公里；较近的恒星相对遥远的背景星，会有极其微小的偏移。知道基线和角度，就能用几何算出距离。1838 年，贝塞尔发表了对天鹅座 61 的视差测量，这是最早被广泛认可的恒星距离直接测量之一。

> 不必抵达群星，也可以丈量群星。

中文副名「隐星视差」也来自这里。标志里那一圈是视场，实心的是开阳，停在缺口里的小星是辅星。

PARALCOR builds software products, AI applications and developer tools, with smart hardware as a longer-term direction.

**The name.** PARALCOR blends PARALLAX and ALCOR. Alcor is the faint companion of Mizar in the Big Dipper. Parallax is the apparent shift of an object against a distant background when the observer moves. Astronomers could not travel to the stars, so they measured them by changing where they looked from: observations six months apart, about 300 million km apart along Earth's orbit, reveal a tiny shift in a nearby star's position, and geometry turns that angle into a distance. In 1838 Friedrich Bessel published the parallax of 61 Cygni, one of the first widely accepted direct measurements of a star's distance.

In the mark, the ring is the field of view, the solid disc is Mizar, and the small star resting in the gap is Alcor.
